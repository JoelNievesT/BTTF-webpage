BACK TO THE FUTURE

Back to the Future

Back to the Future is a Movie from 1985 where a teenager named Marty McFly played by Michael J Fox
is sent to the year 1955 in a Delorean turned to a time machine created by his friend and genius scientist Doc Brown
played by Christopher Loyd. As he is stuck in the past, he finds the younger version of Doc Brown to help him find
his way back home without altering the past.
This movie is truly one of my favorites of all time. I would play it for days at a time.
One of my favorite things of this movie is the characters. Doc is my favorite, to this day i still yell out
"1.21 gigawatts?!" although most people dont understand the reference.

Fun fact!
Did you know that in the early drafts of the films the time machine was supposed to be a refridgerator?
Steven Spielberg ended up replacing the fridge with a DMC DeLorean because of concerns that young people would
lock themselves in a fridge.
